# Changelog

## [1.0.0]

**Features:**
* Packaging choice in the Hyvä Checkout shipping step as a Magewire component, reusing the business rules of the BOXO Reusable Packaging module.
* Packaging artwork on the BOXO cart line in Hyvä Checkout.
* The module disables itself on install when Hyvä Checkout is not present.

**Compatibility:**
* BOXO Reusable Packaging 1.0.0 and up
* Hyvä Checkout 1.3.6 and up
* PHP 8.1 and up
